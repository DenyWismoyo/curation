## 💬 Conversation Playbook

### Initial Discovery Conversation (20-30 minutes)

**Opening** (1-2 min):
```
"Thanks for taking time to chat about assessment challenges.
I'm curious — tell me about your current assessment process.
What's working well? What are the pain points?"

[Listen more than talk — understand their situation]
```

**Problem Amplification** (5-7 min):
```
Based on what they share, mirror back & amplify:

"So if I understand correctly:
- Assessment cycle takes [X] months currently
- Involves [number] of manual steps
- Has [X%] error rate
- Costs about [Rp X] per cycle

And the ideal state would be:
- Faster cycle time
- Fewer manual steps
- Higher accuracy
- Lower cost
- Better compliance

Is that right?"

[Wait for confirmation]
```

**Solution Intro** (3-5 min):
```
"Many institutions we work with face similar challenges.
The good news is, there's a better way.
Omnifit is a platform that automates most of this process.

Let me ask — if we could cut your assessment cycle
from [X months] to [X weeks], and reduce cost by 60%,
would that be valuable?"

[Wait for response]
```

**Deep Dive Based on Interest** (5-10 min):
```
If positive: "Great! Let me show you how..."
[Walk through one of 4 pillars relevant to their main pain]

If hesitant: "What would need to be true for this to work
in your organization?"
[Explore their specific concerns]
```

**Closing** (2-3 min):
```
"Based on our conversation, I think there's real potential here.
What if we set up a 2-hour discovery call with your broader team?
We can dive deeper and validate whether this is a good fit.

How does [date] or [date] look for you?"

[Confirm discovery call]
```

### Objection Handling

#### Objection 1: "We've tried other assessment platforms before"

**Acknowledge & Differentiate**:
```
"I hear you. Let me ask — what didn't work about the last one?
[Listen]

OK, so [specific pain].
That's actually really common with generic platforms.

Here's what makes Omnifit different:
[Choose relevant differentiator from below]

1. Indonesia-first: Compliance, language, cultural understanding
2. Fully integrated: Not another silo, connects to your existing systems
3. Enterprise-grade: Built for scale, not startup/small company tool
4. Custom frameworks: Not template-based, truly customizable
5. AI-powered: Actual insights, not just data collection

Would it be worth 2 hours to explore if we avoid those past mistakes?"
```

---

#### Objection 2: "This seems complicated to implement"

**Acknowledge & Simplify**:
```
"That's a fair concern. Implementation complexity is real.

Here's how we keep it simple:

1. We propose a 2-week POC first
   (Validate with your data, no long-term commitment)

2. Implementation methodology is proven
   (We've done 50+ deployments, have playbooks)

3. Your IT team doesn't do heavy lifting
   (We provide dedicated implementation team)

4. Typical deployment: 12-16 weeks, not 6+ months
   (Because we've streamlined it)

The risk is low — POC costs just 2 weeks of time.
You can validate our approach before committing."
```

---

#### Objection 3: "Budget is tight right now"

**Acknowledge & Reframe**:
```
"I understand budget pressure is real.

Let me ask differently:
- How much are you currently spending on assessment annually? [Rp X]
- How much time does your team spend on it? [X months × X people]
- What's the cost of errors/delays in decisions? [Untold cost]

With Omnifit, you don't ADD budget — you REDIRECT existing budget.

Current annual assessment spend: Rp XXX
Omnifit cost: Rp YYY
Net savings: Rp ZZZ

So the budget conversation becomes:
'We're investing the same amount, but getting 2-3x the value'

Plus: We offer flexible payment terms (monthly installments possible).

Would it make sense to model the ROI for your specific numbers?"
```

---

#### Objection 4: "Security is a concern — we're dealing with sensitive data"

**Acknowledge & Assure**:
```
"That's the RIGHT concern to have. Employee assessment data is sensitive.

Omnifit takes security seriously:

1. Certifications: ISO 27001, SOC2, PDP-compliant
   (Not just claims — third-party audited)

2. Encryption: AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit
   (Same standard as banking systems)

3. Audit trail: Every change logged with who/what/when/where
   (For compliance & employee fairness)

4. Zero breaches: 5 years of operation, zero data breaches
   (That's our track record)

5. Compliance: Ready for government audit
   (Detailed compliance documentation available)

I can connect you with our security & compliance team
to do a deep-dive if that would help."
```

---

#### Objection 5: "We want to see it work for our specific use case first"

**Acknowledge & Propose POC**:
```
"That's exactly how we think about it too.

Let me propose: 2-week Proof of Concept

In that time:
- We set up Omnifit with your data
- We design framework specific to your context
- We test integration with your existing systems
- We validate the approach with your team

No long-term commitment.
Just $X investment for 2 weeks.
You get to see if this works for you.

If you love it: Let's sign full partnership
If you don't love it: Zero hard feelings, we part as friends

How does that sound?"
```
