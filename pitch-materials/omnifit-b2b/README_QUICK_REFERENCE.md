## 📞 Critical Talking Points to Never Miss

1. **Lead with their pain**: Not Omnifit capabilities
   - "Your assessment cycle is 6 months" → Not "Omnifit is smart"
   - "You're spending Rp 150M per cycle" → Not "Our platform is cloud-native"

2. **Always have numbers**: Vague claims don't convince CFO or CIO
   - "60% faster" not "Much faster"
   - "Rp 130M savings" not "Big cost savings"
   - "99.99% uptime" not "Very reliable"

3. **Proof always beats claims**: Show evidence
   - Customer testimonials > Feature lists
   - Case studies > Capability slides
   - "50+ institutions use us" > "We're the best"

4. **Make it personal**: Tailor to THEIR situation
   - Use their institution name
   - Reference their industry/challenges
   - Show similar customer in same sector

5. **Clear next step**: Always end with explicit ask
   - Not: "Let me know if interested"
   - Yes: "I'd like to schedule a 2-hour discovery call. How about Tuesday or Wednesday?"

## 💡 Sales Hacks & Pro Tips

1. **The Anchor Principle**: First number mentioned often sticks
   - Lead with biggest benefit number: "60% faster assessment"
   - Then support with other details

2. **The Credibility Multiplier**: Combine multiple proof types
   - Customer quote + metric + case study = Very credible
   - Instead of just one type of proof

3. **The Pain Amplification**: Ask them to calculate their own pain
   - "How many hours does your team spend on assessment?"
   - "What's the cost of that? (hours × hourly rate)"
   - They realize the pain themselves → more convincing

4. **The Future Pacing**: Paint vivid picture of success state
   - "Imagine your next assessment cycle taking 6 weeks instead of 6 months..."
   - "Picture your team having time for strategic projects..."
   - Emotion + logic = Conversion

5. **The Easy Yes**: Always offer POC option
   - "2-week proof of concept, no long-term commitment"
   - Lower barrier to saying yes
   - 70% of POCs convert to full partnership

**Q: Should I mention competitors?**

A: In Email 3 of sequence, offer: "I can connect you with [Customer] HR head for 15-minute reference call. Would that be helpful?"
