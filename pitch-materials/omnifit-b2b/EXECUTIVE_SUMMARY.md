## 🚀 Sales Process & Timeline

### Phase 1: Discovery (Week 1-2)
**Goal**: Understand prospect pain, validate fit  
**Tactics**: Cold outreach, discovery call, needs assessment  
**Materials**: Discovery email, 30-sec elevator pitch  
**Success**: Agreement to explore POC

### Phase 2: POC Proposal (Week 3-4)
**Goal**: Validate approach, build credibility  
**Tactics**: Proposal meeting, POC scope definition  
**Materials**: Partnership proposal, ROI calculator, case study  
**Success**: POC agreement

### Phase 3: Proof of Concept (Week 5-6)
**Goal**: Prove value on prospect's data  
**Activities**: Framework design, integration testing, pilot  
**Duration**: 2 weeks, minimal commitment  
**Success**: Positive POC results, commercial conversation

### Phase 4: Commercial Negotiation (Week 7-8)
**Goal**: Finalize terms, get approval  
**Tactics**: Commercial discussion, contract review, budget approval  
**Materials**: Detailed proposal, partnership terms, SLA  
**Success**: Signed contract

### Phase 5: Implementation (Week 9-24)
**Goal**: Deliver value  
**Duration**: 12-16 weeks, full deployment  
**Success**: Live system, value realization

**Total Sales Cycle**: 8-12 weeks (first contact to contract)
